Our Story

North Star Bakery started out of a home kitchen, baking loaves for neighbors and farmers market stalls before we ever had a storefront. We opened the doors on Maple Street a few years later and have been baking for this neighborhood ever since.

Sourcing Philosophy

We buy flour and grain from mills within a few hours of here whenever we can, and everything is mixed and shaped from scratch on-site. No par-baked shortcuts, no preservatives.

Staff Highlights

Our head baker has been keeping the sourdough starter alive since day one, and our cake decorator trained at a local culinary school before joining us full time. Most of the crew has been here for years, not months.
