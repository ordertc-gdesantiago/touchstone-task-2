Our Story

How We Started

North Star Bakery began in 2015 when founder Elena Marsh started selling sourdough loaves out of her home kitchen at the Sunday farmers market. What started as a folding table and a dozen loaves grew into a bakery loved by the whole neighborhood. In 2018, Elena opened our storefront on Maple Street, where we still bake everything fresh, every single day.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We partner with Pacific Northwest farms and mills for our flour, dairy, and seasonal fruit. Our sourdough starter is fed daily with organic, stone-milled flour from a family mill outside Salem, and our produce follows the seasons instead of a fixed menu.
