Welcome to North Star Bakery

We're a small neighborhood bakery on Maple Street, baking bread, pastries, and cakes fresh every morning. Stop in for a coffee and a croissant on your way to work, or call ahead and we'll have your order ready when you get here.

What We're Known For

Our sourdough gets a two-day rise before it ever sees the oven, and our croissants are laminated by hand every morning starting at 4 a.m. We also build custom cakes for birthdays, showers, and neighborhood block parties.

Hours & Location

214 Maple Street, open Tuesday through Sunday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed Mondays so the ovens can rest too.

Featured Item of the Week

This week's feature is our braided cinnamon loaf, baked in small batches and usually gone by 10 a.m. on weekends, so get here early or call ahead.
